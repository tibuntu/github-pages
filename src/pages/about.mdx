# About

I'm Timo &mdash; a Cloud Platform Engineer in Oberhausen, Ruhrgebiet,
Germany. I spend my days managing Kubernetes at scale, and my evenings
in a homelab rabbit hole: Raspberry Pi clusters, a Synology NAS,
Home Assistant, and whatever automation project has grabbed my
attention.

## What gets me excited

- **Kubernetes in all its forms.** There's a special satisfaction in
  orchestrating containers &mdash; whether it's battle-tested
  production systems or running Paperless on a Pi in my living room.
  The fundamentals don't change; the scale just does.
- **Home automation done right.** I'm obsessed with making smart homes
  that actually make life easier without the privacy creep.
  [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/) is my daily driver.
- **Open source through and through.** Ubuntu was my first distro, and started my journey into open source and Linux.
  I love open source software and Linux, and believe in building with open tools and giving back to the
  communities that shaped me.
